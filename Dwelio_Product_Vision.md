## 1. Executive Summary

**Tagline:** *Find it. Trust it. Move in.*

Dwelio is Nigeria's first full-stack property marketplace — connecting landlords, tenants, and buyers directly, cutting out middlemen, eliminating fake listings, and making renting or buying a home in Nigeria safe, transparent, and digital-first.

Nigeria's property market is broken. Millions of Nigerians lose time and money every year to agent fees, fraudulent listings, year-long upfront rent demands, and zero digital paper trail. Existing platforms like PropertyPro and NigeriaPropertyCentre are listing directories — they do not solve trust, payments, or the middleman problem.

**Dwelio changes this.** Inspired by the trust-first model of Airbnb, the middleman-disruption of Uber, the rich listings experience of Zillow, and the flexible payments of Rentberry — but built entirely for the Nigerian context — Dwelio launches in **Ibadan** before scaling nationally.

### What Dwelio Does

- 🏠 Landlords list verified properties for free and receive payments digitally
- 🔑 Tenants find, tour, and secure homes without a single agent fee
- 📋 Buyers browse verified sales listings and connect directly with sellers
- 💳 Rent is paid **monthly** instead of 1–2 years upfront
- 📄 Every tenancy agreement is generated, signed, and stored digitally

## 2. The Problem We're Solving

Finding a home in Nigeria today is painful, expensive, and dangerous. Here are the five core problems Dwelio directly addresses:

| # | Problem | Impact |
|---|---------|--------|
| 🧱 | **The Middleman Problem** | Agents charge 10–15% in fees just to unlock a door. Most add zero value — they are a tax on desperation. Dwelio eliminates the need for an agent entirely. |
| 🚨 | **Scams & Fake Listings** | Nigerians lose billions of Naira annually to fake listings and impersonator landlords. Dwelio's ID verification layer makes every listing traceable and accountable. |
| 💸 | **1–2 Year Upfront Rent** | The norm of paying 1–2 years rent in advance is economically devastating for young Nigerians. Dwelio's instalment payment system breaks this cycle. |
| 📄 | **No Digital Paper Trail** | Most tenancy agreements are handwritten, easily disputed, and rarely enforced. Dwelio generates legally structured digital agreements for every tenancy. |
| 🔍 | **No Affordable Discovery** | Finding affordable housing in your target area is near impossible without an agent. Dwelio's AI recommendation engine matches tenants to properties based on budget, location, and lifestyle. |

## 3. The Solution — What Dwelio Is

Dwelio is a two-sided marketplace with three user types, a trust infrastructure layer, a payments engine, and a legal document generator — all in one platform.

### 3.2 The Three User Types

#### 🏠 Landlords / Property Owners
- List properties for rent or sale
- Earn a verified badge after ID check
- Receive digital rent payments monthly
- Generate tenancy agreements instantly
- Track payment history in a dashboard
- Respond to tenant inquiries in-app

#### 🔑 Tenants
- Search listings by location, price, and type
- Watch virtual tours before visiting

- Connect directly with sellers — no agent

## 4. Core Feature Set

### Phase 1 — Foundation *(Months 1–4)*

> Build the core value proposition. Get landlords listing and tenants searching.

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Property Listings** | Landlords create listings with photos, video, price, location, and property type |
| **Advanced Search & Filters** | Filter by state, LGA, price range, bedrooms, and property type (rent / sale) |
| **Landlord ID Verification** | NIN or BVN check to verify landlord identity — shows a verified badge on listings |
| **Virtual Tours** | Landlords upload walkthrough videos; tenants can tour remotely before visiting |
| **In-App Messaging** | Direct chat between landlord and prospective tenant — no WhatsApp needed |
| **User Profiles & Reviews** | Two-sided reviews — tenants rate landlords, landlords rate tenants |

---

### Phase 2 — Payments & Legal *(Months 4–7)*

> This is where Dwelio becomes truly differentiated. No other Nigerian platform does this end-to-end.

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Monthly Rent Payments** | Tenants pay monthly via Paystack or Flutterwave — Dwelio aggregates and pays landlord |
| **Escrow on Move-In** | Funds held in escrow until tenant confirms move-in — protects both sides |
| **Digital Tenancy Agreement** | Auto-generated legally structured agreement based on listing details — both parties sign digitally |
| **Payment Receipts & History** | Every payment logged with a downloadable receipt — full audit trail |
| **Rent Reminder Notifications** | Automated SMS/email reminders for upcoming rent due dates |
| **Wallet System** | In-app wallet for landlords and tenants — faster, cheaper transactions |

---

### Phase 3 — Intelligence & Growth *(Months 7–12)*

> Once the core marketplace is running, these features create lock-in and competitive moat.

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Property Recommendations** | ML model recommends listings based on user behaviour, budget, and location history |
| **Neighbourhood Insights** | Average rent prices, safety scores, and amenities per area in Ibadan, Lagos, Abuja |
| **Property Sales Listings** | Dedicated buy/sell section with mortgage calculator and document checklist |
| **Landlord Analytics Dashboard** | Views, inquiries, conversion rate, and revenue insights for each property |
| **Agent Lite Mode** | Optional verified agent accounts for legitimate agents — with strict fee transparency |
| **Mobile App** | Native iOS and Android apps — core web first, then mobile |

## 5. Revenue Model

> **Core principle:** Dwelio takes a small percentage cut from every successful rent transaction processed through the platform — making money only when users make money.

| Revenue Stream | How It Works | When |
|----------------|-------------|------|
| **Transaction Fee (Core)** | 2.5–5% cut on every rent payment processed through Dwelio | Phase 2 launch |
| **Premium Listings** | Landlords pay to boost visibility, get featured placement, unlock analytics | Phase 2 |
| **Tenancy Agreement Fee** | Small flat fee (₦2,000–₦5,000) per generated & signed agreement | Phase 2 |
| **Escrow Fee** | Small processing fee on escrow-protected transactions | Phase 2 |
| **Agent Subscription** | Monthly subscription for verified agent accounts with lead management tools | Phase 3 |
| **Data & Insights API** | Sell anonymised market data to banks, developers, and government | Phase 3+ |

## 6. Core User Journeys

### 6.1 Tenant Journey — Finding & Securing a Home

```
1. SIGN UP      → Create account with phone + email. BVN optional for trust score.
2. SEARCH       → Filter by area (e.g. Bodija, Samonda), price, bedrooms, type.
3. DISCOVER     → AI recommends matches. Browse photos. Watch virtual tour video.
4. TRUST CHECK  → View landlord's verified badge, rating, and past tenant reviews.
5. INQUIRE      → Send direct in-app message. Schedule physical visit.
6. SECURE       → Pay first month via escrow. Dwelio generates tenancy agreement.
7. MOVE IN      → Confirm move-in. Escrow releases to landlord. Monthly billing starts.
8. REVIEW       → After 6 months, prompted to rate and review the landlord.
```

---

### 6.2 Landlord Journey — Listing & Getting Paid

```
1. SIGN UP & VERIFY  → Register and submit NIN/BVN. Earn verified badge.
2. CREATE LISTING    → Add property details, photos, video walkthrough, monthly price.
3. GO LIVE          → Listing published. Dashboard shows views and inquiry alerts.
4. SCREEN TENANTS   → Review tenant profiles and ratings. Chat in-app.
5. AGREE & SIGN     → Both parties digitally sign the auto-generated agreement.
6. GET PAID MONTHLY → Receive monthly rent to wallet or bank. No chasing required.
```

## 7. Vibe-Coding Roadmap & Tech Stack

### 7.1 Recommended Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Frontend** | Next.js + Tailwind CSS | Fast, SEO-friendly, easy to vibe-code with AI tools |
| **Backend / API** | Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Storage) | Full backend with auth, database, and file storage out-of-the-box |
| **Payments** | Paystack + Flutterwave | Both support Nigeria; Paystack is best for local cards |
| **Maps & Location** | Google Maps API | Listing maps, area search, and directions to properties |
| **Video / Virtual Tours** | Cloudinary or Mux | Optimised video hosting and streaming for property walkthroughs |
| **AI Recommendations** | OpenAI API | Powers smart property matching and search refinement (Phase 3) |
| **Notifications** | Termii (SMS) + SendGrid (Email) | Termii is Nigeria-native; best for OTP and rent reminders |
| **ID Verification** | Dojah or Smile Identity | Nigerian NIN / BVN verification APIs — easy to integrate |
| **Hosting** | Vercel + Supabase | Zero-config deployment; scales automatically |

## 8. Go-To-Market Strategy — Ibadan First

### Why Ibadan Is the Perfect Launch City

Ibadan is Nigeria's largest city by land area — massively underserved by tech-first property platforms. PropertyPro and the others barely have meaningful Ibadan inventory. Dwelio launches here to be a big fish in a less competitive pond, build trust and reviews, then expands to Lagos and Abuja with a working product and real social proof.

| Advantage | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **University density** | UI, Lautech, Lead City, Bowen — hundreds of thousands of students and young professionals constantly searching for housing. Your earliest, most desperate user base. |
| **Lower average rent** | More transactions at lower ticket sizes — perfect for building payment volume and reviews quickly |
| **Landlord culture** | Strong culture of family-owned properties that have never been digitally listed anywhere |
| **Less agent entrenchment** | Lagos agents are organised and territorial; Ibadan's market is more informal — less resistance to your "no agent fees" pitch |

---

### 8.1 Priority Neighbourhoods to Target in Ibadan

| Neighbourhood | Why It Matters |
|--------------|----------------|
| **Bodija** | Middle-class residential hub. High density of landlords with quality properties. |
| **Samonda** | Young professional and student-adjacent. High rental turnover. |
| **Agodi GRA** | Premium properties. Early adopter landlords who want to modernise. |
| **UI Campus Axis** | Ajibode, Abadina, Agbowo — massive student housing demand year-round. |
| **Mokola / Ring Road** | Dense urban core. Affordable housing seekers. Strong tenant base. |
| **Challenge / Dugbe** | Commercial spillover — young workers, small business owners renting nearby. |

---

### 8.2 Getting Your First Landlords (Ibadan-Specific)

- **Facebook Groups** — "Ibadan Properties", "Ibadan Real Estate" groups have thousands of active landlords. Post directly, message landlords already advertising there.
- **Physical canvassing** — Visit estate notice boards in Bodija, Samonda, and Agodi GRA. Knock on doors. Ibadan landlords are approachable.
- **Early mover incentive** — Offer 6 months of free premium listing to the first 100 landlords who verify their identity on Dwelio.
- **Property management companies** — Ibadan has several small PM firms with existing inventory. Partner with them.
- **WhatsApp & Telegram groups** — Landlord groups in Ibadan are active. Pitch with a simple one-pager and a short demo video.

---

### 8.3 Getting Your First Tenants (Ibadan-Specific)

- **University communities** — UI, Lautech, Lead City, Bowen. Students are desperate for trusted housing. Partner with student unions and post on school notice boards.
- **NYSC batch arrivals** — Corps members posted to Ibadan need housing fast every quarter. Be the go-to platform they find when they Google "rent in Ibadan".
- **Content marketing** — Twitter/X and Instagram: *"How to avoid rental scams in Ibadan"*, *"Best areas to rent in Ibadan under ₦200k"*. Pure organic SEO and social traffic.
- **Referral program** — Both referrer and new user get ₦500 wallet credit on first successful booking.
- **Company HR partnerships** — Large employers in Ibadan (Cocoa Research Institute, hospitals, government agencies) have staff who need housing assistance constantly.
